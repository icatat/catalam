## 💍 Wedding FAQ

### When should I arrive?

**Please arrive the day before the wedding.** The wedding day will be long and packed and pulling an all-nighter the day before is not wise. Besides, Oradea is worth exploring, even if just for a day.

### What does the day look like?

**Church ceremony → Photos around the city → Reception at Camelot Resort → Sunrise**

That last part is not a typo. Romanian weddings go until everyone drops. To quote our DJ "I will be at your wedding whether you will be there or not...and I will be playing for as long as one of you is still standing". Come ready for a full, joyful, ridiculous amount of fun. There will be multiple courses of food, toasts, open bar, candy bar, dancing, more food, more dancing, more games. Do not book an early checkout. Do not schedule anything the next morning.

### What should I wear?

**Smart formal** — think suits, cocktail dresses, evening gowns. Romanians dress *up* for weddings and we love that. You won't be overdressed. Most importantly - be you, confident and comfortable in your own skin - just a more refined version.

Early September in Oradea is typically mild-to-warm during the day and cooler once the sun goes down, and part of Camelot is outdoors — so bring a jacket, wrap, or something layerable for later in the evening.

**What NOT to wear:** No full white or ivory — that's the bride's territory and Romanians take it seriously. All-black head to toe is also traditionally associated with mourning. A pop of color never hurts at a Romanian wedding!

### What other traditions should I know about?

**Grăitor** - Don't be alarmed! The **Grăitor** is the leader of the serenade — the head of the groom's party who arrives singing outside the bride's door. It's joyful, it's theatrical, it's the beginning of the party and it's one of our favorite parts of the day. Clap along, cheer, enjoy the spectacle - it will be fun even if you don't speak the language.

**Traditional Dances** — at some point during the reception the dancefloor opens up for Romanian circle dances — the *hora* and others. You do not need to know the steps. Grab the hand of the person next to you and keep moving. Romanian guests will guide you. Jump in.

**The Shoe Game** — we'll each hold one of our own shoes and one of each other's. Questions about our relationship get asked — who's the better cook? Who said "I love you" first? — and we answer by raising the relevant shoe. It's funny, revealing, and the crowd gets very involved - bring your burning question.

### Will I understand what's going on?

Mostly yes! With a mix of local and international guests, we'll make sure key moments are explained as they happen. But even when things are in Romanian, the mood is always universal. Our Romanian guests will be delighted to adopt you for the evening.

### Any useful phrases for the day?

Just one really gets you everywhere:

> 🥂 ***Noroc!*** — pronounced *Noh-ROCK* — means "Cheers!" Use it every single toast, make eye contact, and you will instantly belong.

A few more that will earn you goodwill:
- *Bună ziua* — Good day (formal hello)
- *Mulțumesc* — Thank you (mool-tsoo-MESK)
- *Felicitări!* — Congratulations
- *Casă de piatră!* - "Stone house" - the traditional way to wish the bride and groom a long, happy marriage together
- *La mulți ani!* - "To many years" - a generic greeting used for many occasions, from New Year's Eve to birthdays and weddings
- *Sărutmâna!* - "Kiss hand" - a greeting primarily addressed to the elderly, or a very polite way a man might greet a woman he is not close to

## 📍 The Venue & Getting There

Our reception is at **Camelot Resort** — a lakeside spot with weeping willows and an open-air terrace, in the village of Husasău de Criș (Comuna Ineu). Fair warning: it's **not in Oradea itself**. It's about **18–20 km outside the city (a 20–25 min drive)**, and there's no walk-in or public-transit access, so everyone will need wheels of some kind to get there.

📍 [Camelot Resort on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Camelot%20Resort%2C%20Husasau%20de%20Cris%2C%20Bihor%2C%20Romania)

### How do I get to the venue?

**Don't worry — we're planning group transport from central Oradea so nobody is left stranded.** Details will follow closer to the date. In the meantime, if you'd rather make your own way (or as a fallback), it's an easy 20–25 min drive from the city and your options are:

- **Bolt / Uber** — both operate in Oradea and are the easiest way out to the venue.
- **Taxi** — widely available; ask your hotel for a trusted metered company.
- **Rental car** — great if you're also planning to explore beyond Oradea (more on that below).

### What about getting back at the end of the night?

There's **no set cutoff time** — leave whenever you're ready. Just plan your return in advance: Bolt and taxis do work, but availability can be thin that late out in a smaller village, so it's worth arranging a ride ahead of time or coordinating with other guests heading back the same way.

And if you've got bags or belongings with you and need somewhere to stash them for a bit — **just ask us.** We may be able to keep things in our room, or point you to another option.

## 🛫 Getting to Oradea

### By air

- **Oradea International Airport (OMR)** — the closest option, ~7 km from downtown. Served by [Wizz Air](https://wizzair.com) (Milan Bergamo, Rome Fiumicino, Dortmund — seasonal), [HiSky](https://hisky.aero), [TAROM](https://www.tarom.ro) and [LOT](https://www.lot.com) (Bucharest, Warsaw), and [Lufthansa](https://www.lufthansa.com) (Munich). Routes change with the seasons, so double-check current flights for your dates. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Oradea%20International%20Airport)
- **Debrecen Airport (DEB), Hungary** — ~1.5–2 hr drive, with more regular [Wizz Air](https://wizzair.com) connections around Europe. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Debrecen%20Airport)
- **Cluj-Napoca (CLJ)** — ~2.5 hr drive, Romania's main regional hub with wide international connections ([Wizz Air](https://wizzair.com), [Ryanair](https://www.ryanair.com), [TAROM](https://www.tarom.ro), and more). [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cluj-Napoca%20International%20Airport)
- **Budapest (BUD)** — ~4–4.5 hr drive, but the widest range of international flights of any nearby airport; a great fallback if you can't find a convenient fare into Oradea, Debrecen, or Cluj. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Budapest%20Ferenc%20Liszt%20International%20Airport)

### By bus

[FlixBus](https://www.flixbus.com) (plus regional operators [Infobus](https://infobus.eu) and President Tour) connects Oradea to dozens of destinations:

- **Budapest ↔ Oradea:** ~5–5.5 hrs, from around €15–25
- **Cluj-Napoca ↔ Oradea:** ~2.5–3 hrs, from around €7
- **Bucharest ↔ Oradea:** ~9–10 hrs, from around €20

### By train

Trains run from **Budapest to Oradea (~4 hrs)** and from **Cluj-Napoca to Oradea (~2–2.5 hrs)**. Booking directly through [CFR Călători](https://www.cfrcalatori.ro) (Romania) or [MÁV](https://www.mavcsoport.hu) (Hungary) is usually cheapest.

## 🚗 Getting Around

Oradea's city center is lovely and very walkable. But since the wedding venue is outside town, you'll want a car, taxi, or our group transport for the wedding day itself. Your options:

- **Bolt / Uber** — both operate in Oradea and are the easiest way to get around without renting a car.
- **Taxi** — widely available; use metered local companies (ask your hotel for a trusted one) rather than airport touts.
- **Rental car** — worth it if you plan to explore beyond Oradea (Băile Felix, the Apuseni, or deeper into Transylvania). Major agencies operate at Oradea Airport and in the city center; Debrecen and Budapest airports have a wider selection.

## 💶 Money & Practicalities

**Currency:** Romania uses the **Romanian Leu (RON)** — *not* the Euro. Cards are widely accepted in the city, but smaller places may be cash-only. ATMs are easy to find downtown.

**Language:** Romanian is the main language, but Oradea has a large Hungarian-speaking community, so Hungarian is widely understood too. English is common among younger people and in hotels/restaurants, but not universal — a translation app is a good idea for older locals or smaller shops.

**Power outlets:** Type C/F (European standard, 230V) — bring an adapter if you're coming from the UK, US, or anywhere outside continental Europe.

**Time zone:** Eastern European Time (EET).

**Visas:** Romania is in the EU/Schengen area — check the requirements for your passport. If you need help or recommendations, just reach out to **Catalina**.

## 🗺️ Where to Explore

You've come this far — make the most of it! Here are our top picks before and after the wedding.

### Oradea itself 🏛️

Start with our own [travel blog on Oradea](https://www.catalam.com/blog/oradea) for the full guide to the city.

### Easy day trips near Oradea (10 min – 1.5 hr)

- **Băile Felix** 🧖 — Romania's best-known thermal spa town, just 10–15 min away (about 8 km). Thermal pools, aquaparks (Nymphaea, Felixarium), and the perfect way to recover the day after the wedding. *We love this one — highly recommend if you want to unwind.* [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Baile%20Felix%20Romania)
- **Apuseni Mountains** 🏔️ — hiking, the Pădiș Plateau, and show caves including **Meziad Cave** and **Scărișoara Ice Cave** (one of the largest ice caves in the world). Full-day trips, often done as guided tours from Oradea. [📍 Pădiș Plateau](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Padis%20Plateau%20Apuseni%20Mountains) · [📍 Meziad Cave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Meziad%20Cave%20Romania) · [📍 Scărișoara Ice Cave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Scarisoara%20Ice%20Cave%20Romania)
- **Bear Cave (Peștera Urșilor)** — dramatic stalactites and stalagmites plus ancient cave-bear fossils. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pestera%20Ursilor%20Chiscau%20Romania)

### Further afield in Transylvania (worth it for a multi-day extension)

> 🚙 *A quick note: most of these routes run on two-lane national roads rather than highways, so drive times often run longer than the raw distance suggests. The estimates below build in a realistic buffer — but add extra time for summer weekend traffic or if you're stopping along the way.*

- **Cluj-Napoca** (~2–2.5 hr) 🎓 — Transylvania's coolest, liveliest city: young, vibrant, great food and a buzzing café and nightlife scene. Worth a night or two, especially if you're flying in that way. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cluj-Napoca%20city%20center%20Romania)
- **Turda Salt Mine** (~2.5 hr, near Cluj) — a genuinely spectacular underground salt mine turned amusement park/museum, and one of Romania's most unique attractions. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Salina%20Turda%20Romania)
- **Corvin Castle, Hunedoara** (~3–3.5 hr) 🏰 — one of the largest and most dramatic Gothic-Renaissance castles in Europe. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Corvin%20Castle%20Hunedoara%20Romania)
- **Bran Castle & Sighișoara** (~5–6 hr, further southeast) — the "Dracula's Castle" (yes, really) and a beautifully preserved medieval walled town; best combined into a longer road trip if you have extra days. [📍 Bran Castle](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bran%20Castle%20Romania) · [📍 Sighișoara](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sighisoara%20Historic%20Center%20Romania)
- **Sibiu** (~3.5–4 hr) — a beautifully preserved Saxon old town and one of Romania's prettiest cities. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sibiu%20Old%20Town%20Romania)
- **Brașov** (~5.5–6 hr) — Gothic old town at the foot of the Carpathians, and a great base for Bran Castle and hiking. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brasov%20Old%20Town%20Romania)
- **Peleș Castle, Sinaia** (~7–7.5 hr, ~470 km) — the ornate former royal castle, one of Romania's most-visited sights. It's genuinely far from Oradea on its own, so it only makes sense as part of a multi-day Transylvania loop (paired with Brașov, ~45–60 min away). [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peles%20Castle%20Sinaia%20Romania)
- **Transfăgărășan Highway** (~6+ hr to reach directly, plus drive time) — the legendary mountain road through the Făgăraș range; best accessed from Sibiu or Brașov (~2 hr from either) rather than straight from Oradea, and only realistic on a multi-day loop. Note the high-altitude section is typically closed in winter. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Transfagarasan%20Highway%20Romania)

### Maramureș (worth a dedicated trip, ~4–4.5 hr from Oradea)

A Romanian county where old traditions are still very much alive. If you fancy laughing in death's face, don't miss the **Merry Cemetery (Cimitirul Vesel), Săpânța** — famous for its colorful, wittily-inscribed tombstones. It's a genuinely unique and moving place, not somber despite the name. Usually paired with a night or two in the wider region to see the traditional wooden churches and villages. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Merry%20Cemetery%20Sapanta%20Romania)

### Beyond Romania

- **Budapest, Hungary** (~4–4.5 hr) 🇭🇺 — easily combined with the trip since many guests will already be flying through it. Grand thermal baths, Danube views, and a very walkable historic center. If you haven't been, this is your excuse. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Budapest%20Hungary)
- **Vienna, Austria** (~8 hr from Oradea, or ~4 hr from Budapest) — an elegant add-on if you're already routing through Budapest. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Vienna%20Austria)
- **Timișoara** (~2.5–3 hr) — Romania's elegant "Little Vienna," worth a stop on its own or as a waypoint if you're driving up from Serbia. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Timisoara%20city%20center%20Romania)
- **Coming from Serbia?** — Belgrade to Oradea is about ~5.5–6 hr driving (~440 km), naturally routing through **Novi Sad** and **Timișoara**, both worth a stop along the way. [📍 Novi Sad](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Novi%20Sad%20Serbia) · [📍 Belgrade](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Belgrade%20Serbia)

> 🍿 Tip: for the good, the bad and the ugly, check out [Anthony Bourdain's *No Reservations* — Transylvania episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7uQvfCMnC_k) (unfortunate choice of a guide).

## 🛣️ Road Trip Ideas

Feeling adventurous? Both routes below start and end in Oradea and are best done by rental car. A quick geography note: Sibiu sits *west* of Brașov, so visiting Sibiu before Brașov (rather than after) avoids backtracking — the order below reflects that.

### Route A: Castles & Salt Mines (5–6 days)

Oradea → Cluj-Napoca → Turda → Sighișoara → Sibiu → Brașov → Peleș Castle (Sinaia) → back to Oradea

| Day | Stop | Drive from previous | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Cluj-Napoca](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cluj-Napoca%20city%20center%20Romania) | ~2–2.5 hr from Oradea | Historic center, food & nightlife |
| 2 | [Turda Salt Mine](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Salina%20Turda%20Romania) → [Sighișoara](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sighisoara%20Historic%20Center%20Romania) | ~45 min to Turda, then ~2.5 hr to Sighișoara | Underground salt mine/amusement park; medieval walled citadel |
| 3 | [Sibiu](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sibiu%20Old%20Town%20Romania) | ~2 hr | Saxon old town, one of Romania's prettiest |
| 4 | [Brașov](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brasov%20Old%20Town%20Romania) | ~2–2.5 hr | Gothic old town, base for the day trip below |
| 5 | [Peleș Castle, Sinaia](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peles%20Castle%20Sinaia%20Romania) (day trip from Brașov) | ~1 hr each way (more with Predeal traffic) | Ornate royal castle |
| 6 | Back to Oradea | ~7–7.5 hr direct (or split over 2 days via Cluj) | — |

> *Optional add-on: the Transfăgărășan Highway is ~2 hr from Sibiu or Brașov — worth a detour if you have an extra day and are visiting outside winter, when the high-altitude section is typically closed.*

### Route B: With Bran Castle (5–6 days)

Oradea → Cluj-Napoca → Turda → Sighișoara → Brașov → Bran Castle → back to Oradea

| Day | Stop | Drive from previous | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|

| 6 | Back to Oradea | ~4–4.5 hr | — |

> *Both routes work equally well in reverse, and either can be shortened by cutting a stop if you only have 3–4 days.*
