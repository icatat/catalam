## 💍 Wedding FAQ

### When should I arrive?

**Please arrive the day before the wedding.** The wedding day will be long and packed, and pulling an all-nighter the day before is not wise. Besides, Oradea is worth exploring, even if just for a day.

### When and where should I be ready?

In Romania, the invitation is formally for the **church ceremony and the party** — and, depending on the couple, for the civil ceremony at the city hall. We won't be having a city hall ceremony in Oradea, but we *are* doing the **Grăitor**, the traditional serenade at the bride's door.

That part isn't formally included in the invitation, but we would absolutely love to have you there — especially if you're not originally from Romania and have never seen anything like it.

> ⏰ **Please be ready by 2:00 pm in the city centre.** We'll share the exact location closer to the date. The full running order is on our [itinerary](/romania/itinerary).

### What should I wear?

**Smart formal** — think suits, cocktail dresses, evening gowns. Romanians dress *up* for weddings and we love that. You won't be overdressed. Most importantly — be you, confident and comfortable in your own skin, just a more refined version.

Early September in Oradea is usually mild to warm during the day and cooler once the sun goes down, and part of the venue is outdoors — so bring a jacket or a wrap for later.

**What NOT to wear:** No full white or ivory — that's the bride's territory and Romanians take it seriously. All-black head to toe is also traditionally associated with mourning. A pop of colour never hurts at a Romanian wedding!

### What other traditions should I know about?

**Grăitor** — don't be alarmed! The **Grăitor** is the leader of the serenade, the head of the groom's party who arrives singing outside the bride's door. It's joyful, it's theatrical, it's the beginning of the party, and it's one of our favourite parts of the day. Clap along, cheer, enjoy the spectacle — it's fun even if you don't speak a word of Romanian.

**Traditional dances** — at some point during the reception the dancefloor opens up for Romanian circle dances, the *hora* and others. You do not need to know the steps. Grab the hand of the person next to you and keep moving. Romanian guests will guide you. Jump in.

### Will I understand what's going on?

Mostly yes! With a mix of local and international guests, we'll make sure key moments are explained as they happen. But even when things are in Romanian, the mood is always universal. Our Romanian guests will be delighted to adopt you for the evening.

### Any useful phrases for the day?

Just one really gets you everywhere:

> 🥂 ***Noroc!*** — pronounced *Noh-ROCK* — means "Cheers!" Use it every single toast, make eye contact, and you will instantly belong.

A few more that will earn you goodwill:

- *Bună ziua* — "Good day" (formal hello)
- *Mulțumesc* — "Thank you" (mool-tsoo-MESK)
- *Felicitări!* — "Congratulations!"
- *Casă de piatră!* — "House of stone!" — the traditional way to wish the bride and groom a long, solid marriage together
- *La mulți ani!* — "To many years!" — a catch-all greeting used for everything from New Year's Eve to birthdays and weddings
- *Sărutmâna!* — "I kiss your hand" — a greeting for elders, or a very polite way for a man to greet a woman he doesn't know well

### Is there a wedding registry?

**We have no expectations here.** Having you with us on the day is what we value most, and many of you are travelling a long way to be there — that is already the gift. Please don't feel any pressure to give anything more than your company.

If you would still like to contribute, we've set up a fund to help us start married life with a few unforgettable memories. We're grateful either way.

## 🚗 Getting to the Venue

### Where exactly is Camelot Resort?

Camelot Resort is **not in Oradea itself** — it's a lakeside resort about **18–20 km (a 20–25 minute drive) outside the city**, in the village of Husasău de Criș. It's a beautiful spot — a lake, weeping willows, an open-air terrace — but there's no walk-in or public transport access, so you'll need to sort your own ride out there.

> 📍 [Camelot Resort on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/sY44tKJrQNpnuD7f7)

### How do I get there on the day?

We'll help with transport where we can, but we can't promise a seat for everyone — so please have a plan of your own for the 20–25 minute drive from Oradea. Your options:

- **Bolt / Uber** — both operate in Oradea, and Bolt is the stronger of the two here. The easiest option if you're not driving.
- **Taxi** — plentiful and reliable. Make sure the meter goes on, and ask your hotel to call a trusted local company.
- **Rental car** — the most flexible option and very affordable, especially if you're exploring beyond the city.
- **Share a ride** — coordinate with other guests staying nearby. Most people will be leaving central Oradea around the same time.

### And getting back at the end of the night?

There's no cutoff time — leave whenever you're ready. Just plan your return in advance: Bolt and taxis do work out there, but availability in a small village in the early hours is thinner, so expect to wait around 15 minutes, book ahead, or team up with other guests heading the same way.

If you have bags with you and need somewhere to leave them for a while, just ask us — we may be able to stash them in our room or point you to another option.

## ✈️ Getting to Oradea

### Which airport should I fly into?

- **Oradea International Airport (OMR)** — the closest, ~7 km from downtown. Served by [Wizz Air](https://wizzair.com) (Milan Bergamo, Rome Fiumicino, Dortmund — seasonal), [Lufthansa](https://www.lufthansa.com) (Munich), [LOT](https://www.lot.com) (Warsaw), and [TAROM](https://www.tarom.ro) and [HiSky](https://hisky.aero) (Bucharest). Routes change seasonally, so check what's actually flying on your dates.
- **Debrecen (DEB), Hungary** — ~1.5–2 hr drive, with more regular [Wizz Air](https://wizzair.com) connections around Europe.
- **Cluj-Napoca (CLJ)** — ~2.5 hr drive, Romania's main regional hub with wide international connections.
- **Budapest (BUD)** — ~3.5–4 hr drive, and by far the widest choice of international flights nearby. A good fallback if nothing convenient comes up, and a lovely place to add a couple of days to your trip.

### By bus or train?

Both are comfortable, cheap and very doable.

- **Bus** — [FlixBus](https://www.flixbus.com), plus regional operators [Infobus](https://infobus.eu) and President Tour. Budapest ↔ Oradea is ~5–5.5 hrs (from ~€15–25), Cluj ↔ Oradea ~2.5–3 hrs (from ~€7), Bucharest ↔ Oradea ~9–10 hrs (from ~€20).
- **Train** — from Budapest (~4 hrs) or Cluj-Napoca (~2–2.5 hrs). Booking directly with [CFR Călători](https://www.cfrcalatori.ro) or [MÁV](https://www.mavcsoport.hu) is usually cheapest.

### Do I need a car?

Central Oradea is very walkable, so not for the city itself — but you will need a car, Bolt or taxi on the wedding day either way. A rental is worth it if you're planning to explore beyond Oradea.

## 💶 Money & Practicalities

- **Currency:** Romania uses the **Romanian Leu (RON)** — not the Euro. Cards are widely accepted in the city, but smaller places can be cash-only. Withdraw from a local ATM on arrival rather than using airport exchange booths.
- **Language:** Romanian is the main language, but Oradea has a large Hungarian-speaking community so Hungarian is widely understood too. English is common among younger people and in hotels and restaurants, but not universal — a translation app helps with older locals and smaller shops.
- **Power outlets:** Type C/F (European standard, 230V). Bring an adapter if you're coming from the UK, US, or anywhere outside continental Europe.
- **Time zone:** Eastern European Time (EET).
- **Visas:** Romania is in the EU and the Schengen area — check the requirements for your passport. If you need a letter of invitation or any help with paperwork, [get in touch](/contact) and we'll sort it.

## 💅 Get Glam in Oradea

Romanian ladies are very well taken care of — there is no shortage of hair, make-up and nail salons, and the guys get their share too. Quality is excellent and prices are a fraction of what you'd pay at home.

**Book early.** The best spots fill up fast around weddings, especially the day before — don't assume you can walk in.

> 💵 **Cash reminder:** Many smaller salons prefer, or only take, cash. Bring RON.
