## 💍 Wedding FAQ

### Where is the wedding?

**We are getting married at Axi Plaza on Long Beach, Cam Ranh.** This location is a beautiful beach paradise famous for its pristine white sands, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and world-class luxury resorts. It offers the perfect, peaceful escape from the crowds while being just 15 minutes away from an international airport. There are also plenty of outdoor activities and local restaurants for you to explore.

### When should I arrive?

**Please arrive the day before the wedding if you could.** We are going to host a welcome dinner for all our guests on the evening of Thursday 24 September.

### What happens on the day?

We've got a packed itinerary of love, laughter, and likely some terrible dance moves! We will welcome you at **16:00**, kick off the ceremony at **16:30**, and move straight into cocktail hour and photos by **17:00**. Dinner, speeches, and games start at **18:30**, followed by first dances at **20:30**. Our epic after-party will begin from **21:30** all the way until **2:00**.

### What should I wear?

**Think smart formal, but make it beach-breezy!** Since we'll be celebrating outdoors in the gorgeous (and warm) Cam Ranh sun, breathable fabrics like linen suits for the guys and elegant cocktail dresses or evening gowns for the ladies are your best bets. Most importantly, we want you to feel confident, comfortable, and like the most refined version of yourself.

**What NOT to wear:** Just one golden rule — please leave the white and ivory outfits to the bride.

### Will I understand what's going on?

Don't worry, you won't need a pocket translator to know when to cry or when to cheer! All of our ceremonies, speeches, and activities will be conducted entirely in English.

### What are some useful phrases in Vietnamese?

To get around day-to-day, a polite *"Xin chào"* (Hello) and *"Cảm ơn"* (Thank you) will work wonders.

> 🥂 ***Một, hai, ba, zô!*** — "One, two, three, cheers!" — essential for the bar.

A few more that will earn you goodwill:
- *Ngon quá!* — "Delicious!" — try it when eating, the staff will love it
- *Trăm năm hạnh phúc!* — "To a hundred years of happiness!" — the perfect line for the happy couple

### Will I get sand in my shoes?

As a young man once said, *"I don't like sand, it's coarse and rough and irritating and it gets everywhere."* Our ceremony will be on the lawn, so you won't have to worry about that.

### What is the weather like in Cam Ranh in September?

Late September is the end of the sunny dry season in Cam Ranh. However, it is still very warm and humid. Expect the temperature to be around **25–32°C** throughout the day.

### Is there a wedding registry?

Your presence at our wedding is truly the greatest gift we could ask for, especially since you are travelling so far to celebrate with us in Vietnam! Please do not feel any obligation to give us anything more than your company. However, if you would still like to honour us with a gift, we have set up a small honeymoon fund to help us create unforgettable memories on our first adventure as newlyweds.

> 🎁 [Contribute to our honeymoon fund](https://www.honeyfund.com/site/catalam-2509)

### When do I need to RSVP?

We are very excited to celebrate with you, so the sooner you let us know the better. However, please let us know if you can attend our wedding **by 15 August**.

## 🏝️ Getting There & Where to Stay

### How do I get there?

The closest airport is **Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR)**. It is only a 15-minute drive from Axi Plaza and The Anam Resort. It is about a 2-hour flight from Hanoi, and a 1-hour flight from Ho Chi Minh City.

### Where should I stay?

Right next to Axi Plaza is **The Anam**, a beautiful and luxurious resort. We have also obtained a discount code for our guests throughout the stay — see the booking instructions below.
