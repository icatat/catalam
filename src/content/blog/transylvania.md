# Beyond Oradea

You've come this far — make the most of it.

[Oradea itself](/blog/oradea) is worth a day or two, and we've written that guide separately. This one is about everything *around* it: the thermal spa you can reach in fifteen minutes, the caves and mountains an hour out, and the castles, citadels and salt mines that make a proper Transylvanian road trip.

Whether you have half a day spare or a full week, there's something here for you.

We've pinned everything below on a map so you can see how it all fits together:

> 🚙 **A note on drive times.** Most routes in this region run on two-lane national roads rather than motorways, so journeys take longer than the raw distance suggests. The estimates below already build in a realistic buffer — add more for weekend traffic, or if you're stopping along the way (you will be).

## 🚗 Getting Around

Central Oradea is very walkable and you won't need a car for the city. Everything on this page, though, is easier with one.

- **Rental car** — the most flexible option, and very affordable by Western European standards. Major agencies operate at Oradea Airport and in the city centre; Debrecen and Budapest have a wider selection if you're flying into either.
- **Bolt / Uber** — both work in Oradea. Bolt is the stronger of the two here. Great for the city and short hops like Băile Felix, not for day trips.
- **Taxi** — plentiful and reliable. Make sure the meter goes on, and ask your hotel to call a trusted local company rather than picking one up outside the airport.
- **Guided tours** — a good option for the Apuseni and the caves, which are awkward to reach without a car. Several operators run full-day trips out of Oradea.

## 🧖 Half a Day: Băile Felix

Romania's best-known thermal spa town, only **~10 km / 15 minutes** from Oradea. Warm outdoor pools, aquaparks ([Nymphaea](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CcspYeGjWeGoNg6N9) in the city, [Apollo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ki7MAUrQaBYJpxwt9) and [Aquapark President](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gNwPyw1mqbGwGE5R6) out in Felix), very affordable entry.

*This is the one we'd push hardest.* It is the perfect way to recover the day after a Romanian wedding, and it asks nothing of you but floating.

[📍 Băile Felix on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Baile%20Felix%20Romania)

## ⛰️ A Full Day: Mountains & Caves

### Apuseni Mountains

Hiking, the wide open **Pădiș Plateau**, and some genuinely world-class show caves — including **Meziad Cave** and **Scărișoara Ice Cave**, one of the largest ice caves in the world. Full-day territory, and often done as a guided tour from Oradea if you'd rather not drive the mountain roads yourself.

[📍 Pădiș Plateau](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Padis%20Plateau%20Apuseni%20Mountains) · [📍 Meziad Cave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Meziad%20Cave%20Romania) · [📍 Scărișoara Ice Cave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Scarisoara%20Ice%20Cave%20Romania)

### Bear Cave (Peștera Urșilor)

Dramatic stalactites and stalagmites, plus the ancient cave-bear fossils it's named after. Easier and more family-friendly than the Apuseni caves, and doable in half a day from Oradea.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pestera%20Ursilor%20Chiscau%20Romania)

## 🏰 Further Into Transylvania

Distances below are from Oradea unless stated otherwise.

### Cluj-Napoca — *~2–2.5 hr*

Romania's coolest city: young, vibrant, great food and a buzzing café scene. Worth a night or two in its own right, and an easy stop if you're flying in or out that way.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cluj-Napoca%20city%20center%20Romania)

### Turda Salt Mine — *~2.5 hr, just past Cluj*

A spectacular underground salt mine turned museum and amusement park — Ferris wheel, boating lake, mini golf, all a hundred metres below ground. One of Romania's most unusual sights and an easy add-on to Cluj.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Salina%20Turda%20Romania)

### Corvin Castle, Hunedoara — *~3–3.5 hr*

One of the largest and most dramatic Gothic-Renaissance castles in Europe, and far less crowded than its famous cousin up north.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Corvin%20Castle%20Hunedoara%20Romania)

### Sibiu — *~3.5–4 hr*

A beautifully preserved Saxon old town and one of the prettiest cities in the country. Pastel façades, cobbles, and the famous "eyes of Sibiu" watching you from the rooftops.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sibiu%20Old%20Town%20Romania)

### Sighișoara — *~4–4.5 hr*

A gorgeously intact medieval walled citadel — one of the few in Europe still lived in. Small enough to see in an afternoon, atmospheric enough to justify the detour.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sighisoara%20Historic%20Center%20Romania)

### Brașov — *~5.5–6 hr*

Gothic old town tucked right up against the Carpathians, and the natural base for Bran Castle and mountain hiking.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brasov%20Old%20Town%20Romania)

### Bran Castle — *~1 hr from Brașov*

"Dracula's Castle." Yes, really, it's genuinely there. The Bram Stoker connection is thin and the gift shops are shameless, but the castle is a beauty and the setting is perfect. Go early to beat the coaches.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bran%20Castle%20Romania)

### Peleș Castle, Sinaia — *~7–7.5 hr (~470 km)*

The ornate former royal summer residence, and one of Romania's most-visited sights. Genuinely far from Oradea on its own, so treat it as part of a longer loop — it pairs naturally with Brașov, about an hour away.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peles%20Castle%20Sinaia%20Romania)

### Transfăgărășan Highway — *~6+ hr to reach directly*

The legendary mountain road through the Făgăraș range, hairpin after hairpin. Best reached from Sibiu or Brașov (~2 hr from either) rather than straight from Oradea, and only realistic as part of a multi-day trip. The high-altitude section typically closes in winter, so check before you commit.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Transfagarasan%20Highway%20Romania)

## 🪵 Maramureș — *~4–4.5 hr*

A county in the north where the old traditions are properly kept: wooden churches, carved gates, villages that feel a century older than they are. Worth a dedicated trip rather than a rushed day.

![Cătă and Lam in traditional Maramureș dress under a carved wooden gate](/photo_3.webp)
*Us in Maramureș, in traditional dress under one of the region's carved wooden gates — the gates are a craft in themselves, and every village has its own style. (Dog not included.)*

If you get the chance to try on the traditional clothing, take it. Ours came with a sheepskin coat, and we have never looked better.

The headline sight is the **Merry Cemetery (Cimitirul Vesel)** in Săpânța — famous for its colourful, wittily-inscribed tombstones, where each epitaph is a rhyming verse about how the person lived (and occasionally how they died). If you want to laugh in death's face, this is where you do it. It's unique and quietly moving, and nothing like as grim as the name suggests.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Merry%20Cemetery%20Sapanta%20Romania)

## 🌍 Beyond Romania

### Budapest, Hungary — *~3.5–4 hr by car, ~4 hr by train*

One of Europe's most beautiful cities, and easy to fold into the trip since many people fly through it anyway. Grand thermal baths, Danube views, a very walkable centre. If you haven't been, this is your excuse. [FlixBus](https://www.flixbus.com) and [international trains](https://bileteinternationale.cfrcalatori.ro/en/booking/select/outward) both run the route affordably.

![Cătă and Lam above the Danube in Budapest, with the Hungarian Parliament across the river](/Budapest_2018.jpeg)
*Us in Budapest back in 2018, up on the Buda side with Parliament across the river. Climb up to the Castle District and Fisherman's Bastion for this view — it's the one that sells the city.*

We've written it up properly: **[Budapest in Two Days](/blog/Budapest)** covers both banks, the Ottoman baths, where to eat, and how to carry on to Oradea afterwards.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Budapest%20Hungary)

### Vienna, Austria — *~6 hr from Oradea, or ~2.5–3 hr on from Budapest*

A natural extension if you're already routing through Hungary — much easier as a Budapest add-on than as a trip from Oradea.

![Cătă and Lam at the Christmas market in front of Vienna's City Hall](/Vienna_2018.jpeg)
*Us at the Christkindlmarkt in front of the Rathaus, Christmas 2018 — not what September looks like, but a good reason to come back. The market wraps around City Hall from mid-November, and the Glühwein is non-negotiable.*

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Vienna%20Austria)

### Timișoara — *~2.5–3 hr*

Romania's elegant "Little Vienna" — grand squares, a serious café culture, and a 2023 European Capital of Culture glow-up. Worth a stop in its own right, or as a waypoint if you're coming up from Serbia.

[📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Timisoara%20city%20center%20Romania)

### Coming from Serbia?

Belgrade to Oradea is about **5.5–6 hr driving (~440 km)**, routing naturally through **Novi Sad** and **Timișoara** — both well worth breaking the journey for.

[📍 Novi Sad](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Novi%20Sad%20Serbia) · [📍 Belgrade](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Belgrade%20Serbia)

## 🛣️ Two Ready-Made Road Trips

A road trip through Transylvania is something you won't forget. Both routes below start and end in Oradea and are best done by rental car.

> 🧭 **Geography note:** Sibiu sits *west* of Brașov, so visiting Sibiu before Brașov (rather than after) saves you a lot of backtracking. The orders below reflect that.

### Route A: Castles & Salt Mines — 5–6 days

**Oradea → Cluj-Napoca → Turda → Sighișoara → Sibiu → Brașov → Sinaia → Oradea**

- **Day 1 · [Cluj-Napoca](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cluj-Napoca%20city%20center%20Romania)** — *~2–2.5 hr from Oradea.* Historic centre, food and nightlife.
- **Day 2 · [Turda Salt Mine](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Salina%20Turda%20Romania) → [Sighișoara](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sighisoara%20Historic%20Center%20Romania)** — *~45 min to Turda, then ~2.5 hr on.* Underground salt mine and amusement park in the morning, medieval citadel by evening.
- **Day 3 · [Sibiu](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sibiu%20Old%20Town%20Romania)** — *~2 hr.* Saxon old town, one of Romania's prettiest.
- **Day 4 · [Brașov](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brasov%20Old%20Town%20Romania)** — *~2–2.5 hr.* Gothic old town, and your base for tomorrow.
- **Day 5 · [Peleș Castle, Sinaia](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peles%20Castle%20Sinaia%20Romania)** — *day trip, ~1 hr each way (more with Predeal traffic).* The ornate royal castle.
- **Day 6 · Back to Oradea** — *~7–7.5 hr direct, or split over two days via Cluj.*

> ➕ **Optional add-on:** the **Transfăgărășan Highway** is ~2 hr from either Sibiu or Brașov — well worth a detour if you have a spare day and you're not travelling in winter.

### Route B: With Bran Castle — 5–6 days

**Oradea → Cluj-Napoca → Turda → Sighișoara → Brașov → Bran → Sibiu → Oradea**

- **Day 1 · [Cluj-Napoca](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cluj-Napoca%20city%20center%20Romania)** — *~2–2.5 hr from Oradea.* Historic centre, food and nightlife.
- **Day 2 · [Turda Salt Mine](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Salina%20Turda%20Romania) → [Sighișoara](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sighisoara%20Historic%20Center%20Romania)** — *~45 min to Turda, then ~2.5 hr on.* Salt mine, then medieval citadel.
- **Day 3 · [Brașov](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brasov%20Old%20Town%20Romania)** — *~2–2.5 hr.* Gothic old town, Carpathian views.
- **Day 4 · [Bran Castle](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bran%20Castle%20Romania)** — *day trip, ~1 hr each way (expect tourist traffic in season).* "Dracula's Castle."
- **Day 5 · [Sibiu](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sibiu%20Old%20Town%20Romania)** — *~2–2.5 hr.* Saxon old town, a good stop on the way back.
- **Day 6 · Back to Oradea** — *~4–4.5 hr.*

Both routes work just as well in reverse, and either can be trimmed by dropping a stop if you only have 3–4 days.
